Twenty-one sites. Five thousand homes.

The Common Ground Initiative pipeline. Every site below is under a binding agreement or binding letter of intent. The four-site Launchpad cohort breaks ground in 2026 and delivers 906 homes by 2028.

Notes on dollars.  Full-pipeline estimate applies a blended cost-per-unit of ~$681K to the 5,623-home pipeline — indicative, not committed. Site-level development costs vary by typology, region, and entitlement stage.

01 · Where the sites sit

Eleven cities. One corridor.

Every site in the pipeline sits within the southern Ontario development corridor — concentrated in the Greater Toronto Area, Hamilton, and Simcoe County. National expansion follows the same playbook into Quebec, Alberta, and British Columbia in subsequent waves.
