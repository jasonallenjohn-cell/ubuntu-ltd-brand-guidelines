Twenty years, three moves.

Day 1 · Close

Structure & build

Landowner leases land in via an SPV; draws $19,386,000 cash. BCH + CMHC + City fund the $188.1M build. Non-profit takes 100% ownership of the building.
Years 1–20 · Operate

Run & share

490 homes operate; 147 held at 80% MMR. One $94,048,474 CMHC mortgage at 1.80× DSCR. Cash flow split 60/40.
Year 10 · The pivot

Land secured early

Landowner fully bought out ($16,174,450); non-profit takes fee-simple title a decade early. BCH steps into the 60% seat, is repaid by Year 20 (~$257.3M net non-profit equity).
The affordability earns the financing: 30% at 80% MMR unlocks the CMHC MLI Select terms the whole stack relies on.
