Findings

  1. The 1,000-home concept does not close under Common Ground at the handoff's benchmark inputs, at any land value. Total development cost is $743.4M ($743,389 per home) against stabilized NOI of $15.21M ($15,209 per home). Yield on cost is 2.05% against a 4.25% cap rate. With MLI Select at only 50% of cost the consolidated DSCR is 0.81x (test 1.10x), the weakest phase Year-10 DSCR is 0.89x, the non-profit's worst post-stabilization year is ($1.52M), and the Year-20 takeout is short by ($59.4M). Setting the appraisal to zero does not change the verdict: the supportable Common Ground appraisal is $0.
  2. What has to be true for the stack to close at a $17.0M appraisal. Above-ground hard cost of about $214/sf (benchmark $340/sf, a 37% reduction), or starting rents about 38% above the resale comps, or a combination. DC relief alone cannot fix it: with the affordable units exempt the DSCR only moves to 0.85x; no DC level between $0 and the placeholder makes all four tests pass.
  3. The $17.0M ask can only be a for-sale townhouse number, and even that is not demonstrated. On placeholder costs (Arterial $250/sf on the supplied 313,966 sf GFA, $65k DC per unit, 18% soft, 7% finance, 4% selling, 15% margin) the approved 172-unit condo townhouse plan needs new-build pricing of about $609/sf before it supports any land value: ($28.8M) at $450/sf, ($19.7M) at $500/sf, ($10.7M) at $550/sf, ($1.6M) at $600/sf. Resale comps for 50-year-old stock near the site sit around $410/sf, and Katanna's new-build pricing is VIP-only. As a rental, Arterial's own underwrite of the townhouse plan returned a negative residual. The ask therefore rests on the vendor's for-sale pro forma (lower cost basis or higher pricing than these placeholders), which has not been supplied; Common Ground would in any case be paying a for-sale price for rental land.
  4. Density does not rescue it. Cost per home barely moves with scale ($743,389 at 1,000 homes, $700,491 at 600, $699,108 at 400) because the benchmark is a per-square-foot rate and the suite mix is fixed. Every density case fails all four tests and every conventional residual is negative (600 homes: ($221.9M); 400 homes: ($142.7M)). Fewer homes lowers the absolute shortfall, not the verdict.
  5. Timing helps at the margin; the combined downside is severe. Overlapping phases at 12-month intervals cut TDC to $703.8M (less escalation) and lift DSCR to 0.86x. The combined downside (rents −10%, hard +15%, cap +100 bps, approvals +12 months, leasing −50%) takes TDC to $843.1M, DSCR to 0.69x and BCH mezz outstanding at Year 20 to $236.7M.
  6. Exposure if it were built anyway. Peak cumulative public funding (City + BCH contribution + BCH mezz) is $334.5M; peak MLI Select balance $366.3M; BCH mezz still outstanding at Year 20 $195.5M. The landowner's all-in by Year 10 would be $79.2M on a $17.0M appraisal (draw $10.2M + share $1.9M + buyout $67.1M), which is why the landowner test is not the binding one: the building cannot carry the debt that funds it.

Input quality and next steps

102 inputs; 46 are placeholder, unverified or pending and shown in red on the Inputs tab. The ones that decide the verdict are the hard-cost benchmark, the development-charge placeholder, the resale-listing rents, the missing CMHC median rents, the seller's ask standing in for an appraisal, and the untested lender terms.

  • Obtain a QS Class D estimate on R01 (above-ground, parking, site works, contingency) to replace the $340/sf benchmark; the verdict turns on this line.
  • Obtain CMHC MMR (Oct 2025) and City/Region DC schedules; both are placeholders. Confirm the affordable-unit DC exemption and the enhanced GST rental rebate with tax counsel.
  • Confirm the approval status of Z-2022-12 / S-O-2022-05 and whether the 165-unit revision is what is on file. If the seller is selling entitlement, price it as for-sale townhouse land.
  • Ask the investment team for target IRR, minimum margin and hold period; until then the conventional residuals carry labelled hurdles only.
  • If Common Ground is still wanted here, the credible re-scope is a smaller wood-frame program on the Simcoe frontage with a much lower cost per home, re-run through this model with QS costs.
